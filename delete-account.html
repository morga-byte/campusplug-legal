Delete your account

Campus Plug · Last updated: September 2026

You can delete your Campus Plug account and its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Campus Plug and go to the Account tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete everything to confirm.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, log out, sign back in, and repeat the steps.

If you can't open the app

Email Mwanyama169@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Campus Plug account". We will confirm it is you and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile.
  • Your listings, including their photos.
  • The messages you sent, and your blocked list.

What stays

  • People you chatted with keep their own messages. Your name is replaced with "Deleted user".
  • Reports about content or users may be kept for safety and legal reasons.
  • Listing photos are removed from our image provider within 30 days.
