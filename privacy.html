Privacy Policy

Campus Plug · Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Campus Plug is a student marketplace app operated by Campus Plug ("we", "us") in Kenya. You can reach us at Mwanyama169@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your name (if you sign in with Google, the name Google gives us; you can change it in the app), how you signed in, and the university you choose.
  • Listings: the title, price, category, description, meeting place, and photos you upload, plus when you posted.
  • Messages: the chat messages you send to other users.
  • Safety information: reports you send and the users you block.
  • Technical data: the services we use to run the app (see below) process basic technical data such as IP address, device type and app version.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, or your payment details. Campus Plug does not process payments: buyers and sellers arrange payment between themselves.

How we use it

  • To run the marketplace: show your listings, let students find items, and let you chat.
  • To keep people safe: handle reports, remove harmful content, and prevent abuse.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not show ads.

Who can see what

  • Your listings, including the item details, photos and your display name, are visible to signed-in students on your campus.
  • Your messages are visible to you and the person you are chatting with.
  • Our administrators can technically access data stored in our database. We only look at it to run the service, investigate reports, or when the law requires it.

Service providers

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Sign-In): stores account and app data and handles sign-in.
  • Cloudinary: stores and delivers the photos you upload to listings.

These providers may process data on servers outside Kenya.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app under Account, Delete account. This deletes your listings, the messages you sent, your blocked list and your profile. People you chatted with will see "Deleted user". Listing photos are removed from our image provider within 30 days. Reports about content or users may be kept for safety and legal reasons. You can also ask by email: see how to delete your account.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to how we use it, and receive a copy. Email Mwanyama169@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at odpc.go.ke.

Age

Campus Plug is for university students aged 18 and over. It is not intended for anyone under 18.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your data, including access rules on our database and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure. If you think your account has been misused, email us straight away.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell users in the app.

Contact
